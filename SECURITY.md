# Security Policy

Gantry is on the login path, so security reports are taken seriously, even at hobby pace.

- **Report privately** by email to Mohamed.Hammad@SpacecraftSoftware.org with "Gantry security" in the subject. Do not open a public issue.
- **Acknowledgement:** within 7 days.
- **Scope:** the `gantry` binary, its NixOS module and its packaging. Bugs in greetd, PAM or fprintd belong upstream, but a heads-up is welcome.
- **Supported versions:** the latest release only. Before 1.0, that means the latest commit on `main`.
- **Disclosure:** coordinated. Please allow up to 90 days for a fix before you publish. Advisories are published with each fix.
- **Credit:** reporters are credited in the advisory unless they ask not to be.
