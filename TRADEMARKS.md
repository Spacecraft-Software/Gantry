# Trademark policy

Effective 2026-11-11. Maintainer: Mohamed Hammad, <Mohamed.Hammad@SpacecraftSoftware.org>.

This policy says how you may use the names and logos of the Steelbore and Spacecraft Software projects. It exists so that people can tell the real project from a copy, and so that the project can stay open without anyone being misled about who made what.

## The marks

These names and logos identify the projects and their maintainer. They are claimed as trademarks whether or not they are registered:

- **Steelbore**, **Steelbore OS**, **Steelbore OS Bravais** and **Bravais**
- **Spacecraft Software**
- **Gantry**
- the **Steelbore logo** (the emblem above the name, and the name set in the same lettering)

## Copyright and trademark are separate

The software is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. The Steelbore logo art (`assets/steelbore-logo.txt`, `assets/steelbore-wordmark.txt`, and the SVG they come from) is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Those licenses let you copy, change and share the work. They do not give you any right to use the marks as the name or brand of your own product. That is what this policy covers, and it applies on top of the licenses, never in place of them.

## What you may do without asking

- **Refer to the projects in text.** Write "Steelbore OS" or "Gantry" to say, truthfully, what something is, works with or runs on. Examples: "a theme for Steelbore OS", "tested with Gantry 1.0".
- **Distribute unmodified builds.** You may redistribute official, unmodified releases under their own names.
- **Show the logo to point at the project.** Use the unmodified logo to link to or identify Steelbore OS, with no suggestion that you are the project or are endorsed by it.
- **Discuss, review, teach and advocate.** Non-commercial writing, talks, videos and posts may use the names and logo, as long as they do not claim or imply official status.
- **Parody and commentary.** Allowed if it is clear to a reasonable reader that it is not from the project.
- **Make a minimal remix for non-commercial use.** A lightly changed build (packaging fixes, a patch for your hardware) may keep the name if it is plainly labelled as a modified or unofficial build, for example "Steelbore OS (unofficial build)", and you state what you changed.

Keep the attribution that CC BY-SA 4.0 asks for when you reuse the logo art: the title, the author (Mohamed Hammad), the source, the license (CC BY-SA 4.0 with a link), and a note of any change.

## What needs written permission

Ask first, at the address above, before you:

- use a mark in the name of a company, product, service, package, repository, organisation or domain name;
- sell, or offer as a service, anything under a mark, including merchandise such as clothing, stickers and mugs;
- ship a substantially modified version under a mark (different behaviour, other branding, a different default experience);
- combine a mark with other words or marks into a new mark (for example "Steelbore Cloud");
- use a mark in a way that suggests certification, partnership, sponsorship or official status;
- use a mark to describe a product in a misleading, disparaging or search-ranking-driven way, such as keyword stuffing in titles or metadata.

Permission is given case by case, in writing, and can be withdrawn. It does not carry over to another product or use.

## Derivatives and forks

You may fork and rework the code and the logo art freely under their licenses. If your result is more than a minimal remix, give it its own name and its own logo, and do not use a mark as part of that name. You may say in words that it is "based on" or "derived from" Steelbore OS or Gantry, if true, and you should say it is not an official project.

An adapted version of the logo under CC BY-SA 4.0 is a new work. It may be shared under that license, but it may not be used as the mark of a different product, and it should not be confusable with the Steelbore logo.

## Gantry and the Steelbore logo

Gantry shows the Steelbore logo only when it runs on Steelbore OS, or when `[general] brand = "steelbore"` is set. If you build Gantry for another distribution, leave `brand` at `auto` or set it to `"gantry"`, so that your users see Gantry's own banner and not another project's logo. Distributions that want their own branding can ship per-session banners (`art_dir`) or replace the built-in art, and should use their own marks.

## Fair use and the law

This policy is not meant to limit any use that the law allows you without permission, such as nominative or descriptive use of a name to say what a thing is. Where this policy and the law differ, the law governs. This is a project policy, not legal advice, and it does not by itself register or enforce any mark.
